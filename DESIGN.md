# SmartQuiz — Design Guidelines (v3)

## Direction
Modern fintech-SaaS aesthetic — dark forest-green hero panels, bold black
sans-serif headlines, lime-green pill buttons, soft-shadow white cards.
Confident, product-y, high-contrast.

## Palette
- Background: white (#FFFFFF), soft panel (#F3F6F0)
- Ink (text): near-black (#0D120C), muted ink (#7C8177)
- Dark panel: deep forest green (#132A16 / #0E210F) — hero, CTA banners
- Accent: lime green (#B9F24D) — primary buttons, highlights, chips
- Secondary: forest green tint (#E8F3E2)
- Gold: amber (#FFB020) — highlight badges
- Danger: red (#E5484D)
- Borders: light hairline (#E5E8E0)

## Typography
- Headings: "Sora" — bold, tight tracking, black weight for hero lines.
- Body: "Inter".
- Labels/meta/timers/pills: "JetBrains Mono".

## Visual Language
- No hand-drawn/offset shadows — soft, diffused drop shadows on white cards.
- Buttons are fully pill-shaped (radius 999px), lime-green primary, black text.
- Hero sections are large dark-green rounded panels containing a product
  mockup/dashboard visual, with a "trusted by" logo row beneath.
- Dashboard/mockup cards float with soft shadows, rounded 20px corners.
- Section headers pair a small mono eyebrow with a big bold black headline.
- Badges/pills use soft tinted backgrounds (lime/green/gold) with no hard borders.

## Layout Patterns
- Landing: nav + dark hero panel with mockup, logo strip, feature/solution
  sections alternating white/dark full-width panels, stats + CTA banner, footer.
- Login/Register: dark green illustration panel + white form panel.
- Student flow: card-grid stepper (Semester > Subject > Chapter), pill badges.
- Quiz: centered question card, mono-font timer pill, lime progress bar.
- Result: score ring, stat cards, review list.
- Faculty: sidebar (white) + content panels with manage tables.

## Responsiveness
- Mobile-first grid (1 column mobile, 2-3 columns tablet/desktop).
- Hero collapses to single column with mockup stacked below copy on mobile.
